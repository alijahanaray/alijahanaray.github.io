Section 1 UAP · Apprentices

Registered teacher apprentices working toward licensure. Source: NVF Master student-level extract (1,113 records, May 2021 – May 2026 cohorts). Aggregates only.

Current apprentices by track

276 current apprentices (plus 17 on leave). Track is the program area: Elementary, Special Education, Early Childhood. One record has no track listed.

Graduates by track

Of 743 graduates, 400 have a track recorded; 343 (mostly 2021–2023 cohorts) do not. Percentages are of graduates with a known track.

Who the apprentices are

All 1,113 apprentices. 83.8% women; average age at start 35.4; average UNLV cumulative GPA 3.56. Graduates: average GPA 3.67, median 11.9 months to graduate.

Average GPA by group

Latest UNLV cumulative GPA. Graduates 3.67 · current apprentices 3.58 · ELEM 3.61 · ECE 3.54 · SPED 3.52.

Graduate profile: who they are

All 743 graduates. 84.3% women; average age at start 35.6; average GPA 3.67; 731 (98.4%) hold a license number.

Graduate profile: time to graduate

Months from start date to end date. Median 11.9 months, mean 13.3. 56% finish within a year; 97% within 18 months.

Graduates by Nevada high school county

446 graduates with a matched Nevada high school; 297 attended out of state, had a GED, or had no school listed.

High school locale (all apprentices)

NCES locale of the matched Nevada high school (659 apprentices). 15% come from rural areas or towns.

All apprentices by Nevada high school county

Where apprentices attended high school, from Nevada school files (659 with a county). 374 attended school out of state or could not be matched; 70 had a GED or no school listed. Not the employing district — see data gaps below.

High school sector: public, charter, private

Of 670 apprentices with a matched Nevada high school: 658 public (24 charter), 12 private.

UAP data still needed for the dashboard

  • Employing district: district and public/charter status of the school where each apprentice works (the extract only has entry high school).
  • Coaching contacts: meetings with coach or support specialist per semester.
  • Financials: spend per current student and per licensed graduate.
  • RAPIDS: number registered and number completed.
  • Professional development: completer percentage and satisfaction by cohort.
  • Job placement: licensed graduates employed, by district.

Section 2 Pre-UAP · Rebel Start Academy

Pre-apprenticeship pathway preparing candidates to enter the apprenticeship. Source: Salesforce attribute extract (394 records, Jan 2024 – May 2026 cohorts).

Status of all pre-apprentices

268 active (264 current, 4 on leave); 126 former: 48 withdrew, 39 discontinued, 36 separated, 1 graduated, 2 unspecified.

Where pre-apprentices go

7 Rebel Start Academy participants also hold a UAP apprentice record (matched by ID; 3 are current apprentices, 1 graduated). A clear "completed pre-apprenticeship" status is not recorded in Salesforce — most leavers are coded Separated, Withdrew, or Discontinued.

Year 2 scorecard, satisfaction, and findings

Satisfaction and readiness

Targets: PD 75%, coaching 73%. Exit survey n = 43. Source: CREA surveys, NVForward end-of-program survey.

Key findings

  • Recruitment: 13 information sessions (target 7); 24 social media posts (target 12).
  • Training: 40+ hours of RTI. Self-efficacy gain of +0.42 vs. comparison not statistically significant (p = .119).
  • Support: Coaching satisfaction 86.4% (target 73%). Alumni described support as thinner after graduation.
  • Research: 2 conference presentations, 3 manuscripts, 2 policy papers. Survey response rate 67.7% (target 40%).
  • Bilingual completers: 51 Year 2 completers (31%) are bilingual or multilingual (Hawkins measure c).
