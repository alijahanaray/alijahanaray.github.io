Highlights

The pipeline grew and held together. Enrollment beat target by 50% and the graduation rate cleared its target by 21 points. Employer agreements cover 10 of Nevada's 17 districts.

Apprentices rate the program highly. Every satisfaction measure cleared its target, and 93% of exiting graduates felt prepared to teach.

Retention and placement remain the open items. Graduates have not yet taught a full year, and district employment records are still pending.

Goal 1: Recruitment and the Apprenticeship Pipeline

Every recruitment output met or beat its target. Social media doubled its goal, and information sessions nearly doubled theirs.

The graduating class mirrors the enrolled population. 60.4% of apprentices are from a racial or ethnic minority group, and no group's share of graduates is more than four points off its share of enrollment.

Goal 2: Professional Growth and Apprentice Support

Every satisfaction and completion rate cleared its target. Coaching satisfaction rose to 86.4% against a 73% target.

Goal 3: Retention and Early-Career Support

Licensure is high and consistent across groups; retention is not yet measurable. 170 of 177 graduates hold a Nevada license. Guides on Demand reached 100% of alumni.

Goal 4: Research and Dissemination

Every dissemination target was met or exceeded. Manuscripts tripled their target.
