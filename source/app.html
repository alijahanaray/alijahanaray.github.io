Key findings

  • Pipeline: 501 enrolled; 177 graduated, 276 still enrolled, 17 on leave, 31 withdrew. Median time to graduate 15.7 months.
  • Training: 40+ hours of RTI. Self-efficacy gain of +0.42 points vs. comparison group was not statistically significant (p = .119).
  • Support: Coaching satisfaction 81% (target 73%). Alumni described support as uneven and thinner after graduation.
  • Research: 2 conference presentations, 3 manuscripts, 2 policy papers shared with legislators.

Open items before Year 3 reporting

  • Retention: Not yet measurable; Year 2 graduates have not completed a first year as teachers of record.
  • Placement: Not reportable; district employment records unavailable.
  • Bilingual completers: Not reportable; indicator absent from the data extract.
  • Information sessions: 5 held vs. target of 7. Post-survey response: 31.1% vs. target of 40%.
  • Data notes: Enrollment is 501 in the outcomes extract, 499 in the master file, and 474 in the Hawkins status chart; the latter should be updated. 30 possible duplicate records need resolving before counts are final.
